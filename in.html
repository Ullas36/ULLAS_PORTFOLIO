ORBISQuantum hardwareOrbital Remediation Quantum Intelligence System: choosing and sequencing debris to remove.

Screens 15,982 tracked objects from Space-Track using SGP4 propagation, then runs a 72-hour conjunction analysis with a KD-tree and the Chan 2008 collision probability method. Targets are picked by a diversity-aware score across orbital regimes.

A physics-informed network, DeltaVNet, approximates manoeuvre delta-v. Sequencing is written as a QUBO and solved with QAOA, against a simulated annealing baseline.

n=4, 16 qubits100%
n=5, 25 qubits110.5 ± 9.7%
n=6, 36 qubits115.9 ± 18.7%
n=8, 64 qubits195.1 ± 12.2%

Recorded QPU results by problem size, with the n=4 run as the 100% reference. Hardware noise grows with circuit depth, and quality drops sharply at the larger sizes.

Galaxy spectroscopyAstronomyEnd-to-end analysis of SDSS photometry and spectra for 15 galaxies.

Reads SDSS five-band frames and coadded spectra, fits Gaussian profiles to Hβ, [OIII], Hα and [NII] by Levenberg-Marquardt least squares, and refines each redshift from the median line offset. The Balmer decrement gives dust extinction, and the de-reddened fluxes feed a BPT classification (Kauffmann 2003 and Kewley 2001 lines) into star-forming, composite or AGN/LINER.

Planck18 distances then give star formation rate from Hα and gas-phase metallicity from the N2 index. A separate stage builds pseudo-RGB composites and catalogues sources with photutils background modelling and deblending. The star or galaxy flag is a rule-based check on ellipticity and size, not a trained model.

Hubble RGB compositesAstronomyFrom a MAST archive query to aligned infrared colour images.

Queries the MAST archive through astroquery for Hubble WFC3 infrared flat-field frames (Proposal 15952, filters F127M, F139M and F153M), downloads the calibrated files, aligns the bands to sub-pixel accuracy with Fourier phase cross-correlation, and stacks them into a colour image. Alignment corrects translation only.

Invisible infrared wavelengths are mapped to colour by order: F153M to red, F139M to green, F127M to blue. A batch script groups and processes every downloaded set, and the worked example is Andromeda.
